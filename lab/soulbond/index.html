SOUL BOND

Entanglement swapping · two sigils that never met become a Bell pair

Four qubits. A and A′ share Φ⁺; B and B′ share Φ⁺. A Bell measurement on A′ and B′ (the “officiant”) projects A and B into a Bell pair even though they never interacted. That is entanglement swapping (Żukowski, Zeilinger, Horne, Ekert, 1993). It still needs a classical announcement of the Bell outcome so A and B can apply corrections and confirm the bond. CHSH on A⊗B is computed exactly on the simulated state. This is not a metaphysical soul-tie.
