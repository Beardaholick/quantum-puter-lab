RITUAL CIRCLE

GHZ coven · Mermin inequality · one word collapses the circle

A GHZ state is (|00…0⟩ + |11…1⟩)/√2. Z measurements all agree; the X-parity is fixed; the Mermin operator Re ⟨∏(X+iY)⟩ equals 2N−1, against a classical bound 2⌊N/2⌋ (for N=3: 4 vs 2). That is a Bell theorem for N parties — not telepathy, and not a group mind.
