ASTRAL TELEPORT

Bennett et al. teleportation · mid-protocol Kraus noise on the Bell pair

Idle. Set a state, then run.

Bennett, Brassard, Crépeau, Jozsa, Peres, Wootters (1993). Alice and Bob share a Bell pair. Alice jointly measures her unknown qubit ψ with her half of the pair, and phones Bob two classical bits. Bob applies X and/or Z and holds ψ. Nothing material moves; the original ψ is destroyed by Alice's measurement (no-cloning). After the Bell pair is shared — and before Alice’s Bell measurement — a depolarizing Kraus channel acts on Bob’s qubit: K₀=√(1−3p/4) I, K₁,₂,₃=√(p/4) {X,Y,Z}. That is mid-protocol noise on the entangled resource, not a fog painted on at the end. It is equivalent to Λ(ρ)=(1−p)ρ + p I/2 on the teleported state, so the average fidelity is 1 − p/2. At p=0 the four Kraus collapse to I and F=1 exactly. Decoherence is a real noise model, not a mystical veil.
