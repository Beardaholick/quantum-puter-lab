TIME CRYSTAL

A kicked Ising chain with period-doubled magnetization · a Floquet toy, not a clock made of time

A discrete time crystal is a periodically driven (Floquet) many-body system whose observables repeat every two (or more) drive periods — they spontaneously break the discrete time-translation symmetry of the drive. This toy is a 1-D chain of 12 classical Ising spins with a near-π kick, Ising coupling, and on-site disorder: a cartoon of the Yao / Else–Bauer–Nayak picture, not a laboratory many-body localized chain, and not a crystal “made of time.”
