ZENO BINDING

Frequent observation freezes a precessing qubit · a real quantum effect

A qubit starts in |0⟩ and would rotate toward |1⟩ under H = (Ω/2)σx (Rabi period 1 in these units). If we projectively measure “still in |0⟩?” every τ, the escape probability per look is sin²(Ωτ/2) ≈ (Ωτ/2)². n = t/τ looks give survival ≈ [cos²(Ωτ/2)]ⁿ, which → 1 as τ→0. That is the quantum Zeno effect — sometimes nicknamed a watched pot. It is not a curse; it is measurement back-action.
