QUANTUM TAROT

Entangled three-card spread · CHSH between Past and Future · narrative overlay

The cards are sampled from a simulated 12-qubit state whose rotations and CNOTs are hashed from your question. Past and Future are read from two qubit pairs; their CHSH value S is a real correlator of that state (classical |S|≤2, quantum up to 2√2). The sentence under each card is a symbolic gloss, not a prediction.
