GRIMOIRE NETWORK

Shared sigil phrases as nodes · entanglement swapping weaves the bonds · monogamy is the law

Click two nodes to attempt a soul-bond (Bell swap of their threads). A node that has already spent its thread cannot form a second maximal bond — entanglement monogamy. Re-spin a thread to break a prior bond and try again.

Four nodes, eight qubits. Node i holds a keep qubit ki and a thread ti, prepared in Φ⁺. A “bond” is entanglement swapping (Żukowski et al., 1993): a Bell measurement on titj, plus Pauli corrections on kj, leaves kikj in Φ⁺ even though those qubits never interacted. CHSH on a bonded pair reaches the Tsirelson bound 2√2; on an unbonded pair it stays at the product-state value 0. A qubit already maximally entangled with one partner cannot be maximally entangled with another (Coffman–Kundu–Wootters monogamy). Phrases only label the nodes and tint the drawing; they do not conjure a metaphysical web. This is the same swap as Soul Bond, laid out as a small network.
