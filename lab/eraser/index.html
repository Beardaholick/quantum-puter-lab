QUANTUM ERASER

Delayed choice · fringes return only in coincidence · no signal backwards

A photon at a double slit, with a partner (the “idler”) that can mark which slit. If the which-path mark is kept, the screen is a smooth lump — no fringes. If a later, even space-like, measurement of the idler erases that mark (a complementary basis), coincidences with one idler outcome show fringes, the other anti-fringes. Unsorted, the screen is still smooth. You cannot use this to send a message into the past: without the idler record, there is nothing to see. Wheeler’s delayed choice, Scully’s eraser — simulated here as a textbook wave model.
