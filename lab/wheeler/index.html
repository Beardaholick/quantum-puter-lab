IT FROM BIT

Wheeler’s delayed choice · a late question settles an earlier path · not consciousness creating the cosmos

A quantum is sent through two slits at t=0. Only later, at time τ, do you choose whether to ask “which path?” or “what is the interference?” The screen record that then appears is the one consistent with that late question — even though the slits are already in the past of this spacetime cartoon. That is Wheeler’s delayed-choice experiment. His slogan “it from bit” is an interpretation: information and questions enter the account of what we can say. The mathematics is ordinary quantum mechanics. This page does not claim that human consciousness creates the universe, and it does not send a signal into the past.
