LIVE CYMATICS

Chladni plate driven by your microphone or a tone · wave physics, not quantum

A square Chladni plate has modes sin(nπx)sin(mπy). Here the loudest FFT bins pick (n,m) pairs and we plot the squared mode sum — bright sand gathers at the nodes (near-zero displacement), a fair cartoon of a driven plate. Microphone access is optional; if it is denied, the oscillator still works. No quantum mechanics is involved.
