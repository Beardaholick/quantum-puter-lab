LEY LINES

A split-step Hadamard walk on a lattice · interference draws diagonal ley lines a classical walker never traces

A split-step Hadamard walk on a 2-D lattice: apply a Hadamard (or RY(θ)) to the x-coin and shift in ±x, then a Hadamard to the y-coin and shift in ±y. Interference is ballistic (spread ~ t) and typically bright along the diagonals — unlike a classical random walk (spread ~ √t, a central lump). This is standard walk mathematics, used in quantum algorithms and (in related forms) topological pump literature. It is not a map of mystical earth energy.
