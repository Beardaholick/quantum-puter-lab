MANY-WORLDS DECISION

A branching statevector for three choices · inhabit one world, the rest stay weighted

Each choice is a qubit rotation. Bias the coin; the branch widths are |amplitude|². Click a leaf to inhabit that world.

This is the mathematics of a three-qubit product state (or a slightly entangled one if you fork). Many-worlds is an interpretation of the Schrödinger equation: every branch “stays.” Collapse is another interpretation of the same numbers. We do not prove which story is true. We only draw the tree of amplitudes.
