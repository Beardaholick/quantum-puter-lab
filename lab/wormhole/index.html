WORMHOLE

A 7-qubit analogue of traversable-wormhole teleportation · not a spacetime throat

A 7-qubit circuit analogue of traversable-wormhole teleportation, in the spirit of Gao–Jafferis–Wall (2016/17) and the Google 2022 SYK-inspired experiment (Nature 612, 51). The two “mouths” L and R are three-qubit registers prepared in a thermofield-double stand-in: three Bell pairs. A message qubit is swapped into L after U†, so it is injected “in the past” of a short scrambling unitary U; a two-sided coupling ∏ exp(−i g ZLi ZRi / 2) is applied; U† then runs on R. If the coupling is tuned, part of the message reappears on R0 and F(R0, ψ) rises above the ½ noise floor — size-winding / Hayden–Preskill-style reconstruction on a handful of qubits. This is not a spacetime wormhole, not an Einstein–Rosen bridge, and not a gravity simulation beyond a very small circuit caricature. The curve F(g) is exact for this statevector, not a marketing graphic.
