What this is

Quantum Puter Lab is a local, installable app built from the Quantum Sigil & Resonance Engine and its laboratory. Everything you see is a classical simulation of cited mathematics, dressed in gold, cyan, and film-grain. It is generative / symbolic art, not a supernatural instrument.

On an iPhone

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. The gold icosahedron becomes the app icon. After the first load, the laboratory is cached for offline use.

On a Mac, you can also double-click Quantum Puter Lab.app in ~/QuantumPuter/dist/ for a chromeless window, or serve this folder on your Wi-Fi with python3 serve.py and open the printed URL on your phone.

Limits

There is no real quantum processor here. Chrome or Safari is required for some WebGL and audio features (the sigil’s Three.js view, cymatics). The service worker caches this origin only. Live Cymatics may ask for a microphone. Light Lab and UFO / Propulsion remain separate Python wings in the original project folder and are not bundled in this app.

Physics pointers: CHSH and Tsirelson’s bound, Grover amplitude amplification, Bennett et al. teleportation with mid-protocol Kraus noise, entanglement swapping (Żukowski et al.), BB84, a small-circuit analogue of Gao–Jafferis–Wall / Google 2022 wormhole teleportation (not a spacetime throat), and Zurek’s quantum Darwinism in a toy model.

Everything runs on this device as a classical simulation of the cited mathematics. Aesthetic: obsidian, gold, cyan, film-grain — symbolic, not supernatural.
